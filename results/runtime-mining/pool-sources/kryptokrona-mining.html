Mining

Kryptokrona is a cryptocurrency that utilizes proof-of-work mining to establish distributed consensus. The information and resources provided below will assist you in getting started with mining. Please note that the Kryptokrona Project does not endorse any specific pool, software, or hardware; the content presented here is for informational purposes only.

A core tenet of Kryptokrona's philosophy is to promote equitable mining opportunities for everyone. To that end, Kryptokrona uses CryptoNight-Turtle, a lightweight, ASIC-resistant proof-of-work algorithm. Because specialized ASIC hardware gains no advantage, anyone with an ordinary computer can compete on equal footing — and CPU mining is significantly more efficient than GPU mining here.

The recommended way to mine is p2pool — a decentralized mining pool that combines the steady, regular payouts of a pool with the trustlessness of solo mining. There is no pool operator and virtually no fees: your rewards are paid directly to your own wallet, automatically. It is the fairest and most decentralized way to mine XKR, and the best place to start.

Frequently asked content

Have a different question and can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Reach out to us on Discord and our community will surely have an answer for you.

What is Kryptokrona mining and how does it work?
Kryptokrona mining is the process of using your computer's processing power to validate transactions and maintain the network. Miners solve complex cryptographic puzzles, and in return, they are rewarded with Kryptokrona coins. This process secures the network and ensures the integrity of transaction records.
What is the recommended way to mine Kryptokrona?
We recommend p2pool. It's a decentralized mining pool that gives you the steady, regular payouts of a pool combined with the trustlessness of solo mining. There is no operator and virtually no fees, and your rewards are paid directly to your own wallet, automatically. You run one small program alongside your miner — see the p2pool guide at docs.kryptokrona.org/mining/p2pool to get started. Traditional, centralized pools are also available as a simpler alternative.
What are the requirements to start mining Kryptokrona?
You need a Kryptokrona wallet address, a mining program that supports the CryptoNight-Turtle algorithm (such as XMRig), and a Kryptokrona node to connect to. The recommended setup is to run p2pool and point your miner at it, so your rewards go straight to your wallet. Alternatively, you can point your miner at a traditional pool.
How can I set up my mining rig for Kryptokrona?
Download and install a miner compatible with the CryptoNight-Turtle algorithm (such as XMRig), then configure it with your Kryptokrona wallet address. For the fairest, most decentralized experience, run p2pool and point your miner at it; the p2pool guide walks you through it step by step. If you'd rather keep things simple, you can instead point your miner at a traditional mining pool.
Is it profitable to mine Kryptokrona?
The profitability of mining Kryptokrona depends on various factors, including the efficiency of your mining rig, electricity costs, and the current market value of Kryptokrona. You can use online mining calculators to estimate your potential earnings.
What is p2pool, and why is it recommended?
p2pool is a mining pool with no operator. Instead of connecting to someone else's pool server, you run a tiny pool of your own next to your miner, and all p2pool users worldwide are linked into one shared network. You get the steady payouts of a big pool, but nobody ever holds your coins — rewards are paid directly to your wallet, there are basically no fees, and there's no trust required. It's the fairest, most decentralized way to mine XKR.
What is a traditional mining pool, and should I use one?
A traditional mining pool is a group of miners who combine their computational power on an operator's server to earn more consistent rewards, which are then distributed among members. Pools are simple to join, but they are run by third parties, may charge a fee, and hold your rewards until you withdraw. p2pool is the decentralized alternative that gives you the same steady payouts while paying you directly and trustlessly — which is why we recommend it first.
How does Kryptokrona ensure fair mining opportunities?
Kryptokrona uses the CryptoNight-Turtle algorithm, a lightweight, ASIC-resistant proof-of-work that is designed to be more equitable than algorithms like Bitcoin's SHA256. It allows competitive mining on a variety of everyday devices, including less powerful hardware, providing fairer access to mining for individuals.
