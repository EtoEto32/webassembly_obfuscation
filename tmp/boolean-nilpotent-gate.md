# Boolean nilpotent gate replacement: construction and novelty gate

2026-10-12. **Not adopted: direct composition (Level 3 assessment).**
This was examined as a different mechanism from precision-increasing residual
feedback. No new Wasm, SHA implementation, SMT run, or runtime test was made.
The result below is an algebraic derivation and primary-source comparison.

## Proposed rule

For any finite acyclic word computation with node functions f_i, introduce two
words u_i,z_i per node. External inputs remain fixed. On every synchronous
update, compute all right-hand sides from the old arrays:

```
g_i = f_i(u_predecessors, external_input)
a_i = u_i XOR g_i
u'_i = g_i XOR (a_i AND z_i)
z'_i = a_i AND NOT z_i
```

Every operation is a fixed-width word operation. f_i may include rotations,
right shifts, additions or other deterministic total functions. This rule
does not require the 2-adic congruence-preservation condition of the earlier
candidate. The readout is u at the source output node(s).

## Exact proof and limits

When g is fixed, use the local error coordinate a=u XOR g. Then the update is

```
N(a,z)  = (a AND z, a AND NOT z)
N^2(a,z)= (0, a AND z)
N^3(a,z)= (0, 0).
```

These identities hold bitwise at any word width. Thus u becomes g after two
updates and remains g, while z is zero after three. If a node has source depth
l, induction over depth gives its correct u by time 2*l: after its predecessors
have settled, g is constant and two more updates suffice. All auxiliary words
are zero by 2*d+1 for depth d>=1; the initially proposed 3*d bound is also safe.
This proof makes no claim that g is already correct during the transient.

The first update can also be written u'=mux(z,u,g), with bitwise selection.
The extra coordinate only delays the adoption of the ordinary node value.
An arbitrary initial state is allowed; a precomputed final root is not needed.

Synchronous execution is essential to this proof. Fair asynchronous updates
need not converge: starting N at (1,1), repeatedly updating coordinates
a,z,z gives (1,1)->(1,1)->(1,0)->(1,1). Both coordinates are updated infinitely
often. This is a symbolic counterexample, not an executed concurrency test.

## Concrete prior construction

Gadouleau and Richard, *Simple dynamics on graphs* (2015),
https://www.i3s.unice.fr/~richard/PUBLICATIONS/JOURNALS/2015_Simple.pdf ,
Theorem 6, printed page 16, gives an explicit Boolean nilpotent construction.
Choose its loop-less G to be the two-vertex bidirectional graph on a,z and
choose S0=I={z}. Its negative self-loop at z and positive self-loop at a give
exactly N_z=NOT z AND a and N_a=a AND z. Thus the local dynamical rule above
is an actual two-vertex instance of the published construction, not merely
another system covered by an abstract convergence theorem. The extraction
drops overbars; the adjoining negative/positive-loop description confirms
the signs. The primary PDF text was inspected; a web screenshot attempt failed.

The full candidate is then generated by three explicit operations:

1. Instantiate that known two-coordinate nilpotent network.
2. Shift its constant attractor from (0,0) to (g,0) by XOR on its first
   coordinate, using the original node function for g.
3. Place these local blocks along the unchanged source DAG; sum the local
   stabilization delays along paths.

These operations account for the whole proposed generator. The layer induction
does not require a new global coupling invariant: blocks only consume predecessor
readouts. Consequently, the fact that their targets change before upstream
settling adds no independent mechanism. This is the basis for the Level 3
assessment, rather than a claim that all conjugate dynamics are unoriginal.
No assertion is made that this exact Wasm compiler was previously published.

## A real distinction that does not rescue novelty

Backes and Riedel, *The Synthesis of Cyclic Dependencies with Boolean
Satisfiability*,
https://www.mriedel.ece.umn.edu/wiki/images/d/df/Backes_Riedel_The_Synthesis_of_Cyclic_Dependencies_with_Boolean_Satisfiability.pdf ,
printed pages 8–9 (PDF indices 7–8), require their combinational circuits to
resolve every ternary unknown under controlling-value propagation. In this
two-coordinate gadget, unknown a,z remain unknown under that gatewise analysis.
Binary synchronous cancellation is therefore not identical to their chosen
combinational semantics. However, the explicit nilpotent network above already
provides precisely that synchronous mechanism. Difference from one comparator
does not establish novelty against another closer construction.

## Research consequence

Do not implement a complete hash for this rejected gate wrapper, swap in a
larger nilpotent gadget, change its attractor encoding, or claim a new principle
from removing the earlier candidate's congruence restriction alone. A future
related proposal would need an independent construction beyond locally moving
known attractors and composing blocks in source order.
